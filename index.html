ChatGPT Quota Enforcement — Responsible Disclosure

BugcrowdManual TestingRate-Limit Analysis
Challenge
Investigate how ChatGPT's quota enforcement behaved at the UI level, to check whether restricted features could remain briefly accessible after usage limits were reached.
Approach
Tested the timing between the interface's quota enforcement and the backend state, then submitted the finding through Bugcrowd's responsible disclosure program for review.
Learning outcome
Confirmed by Bugcrowd as a known issue (VRT: Insufficient Rate Limiting) — reinforcing the value of structured, responsible vulnerability reporting on real-world platforms.
